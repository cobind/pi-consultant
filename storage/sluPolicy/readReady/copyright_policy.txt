Copyright

Faculty, staff and students should be familiar with copyright law as it applies to educational uses of materials and the copying of textbooks, literary works, music, recordings, software, and pictorial works. Southeastern’s Copyright Guide summarizes what is and is not copyrightable and gives some general guidelines for understanding Copyright and Fair Use in a university environment. The guide is located at: http://selu.libguides.com/content.php?pid=599976.
