Responsible Administrator: Athletic Director Responsible Office: Athletics Effective Date: 10/31/2023 Approved by: President Date of Revision: 08/01/2026 Tailgating Policy Policy Statement This Policy is to guide all members of Southeastern Louisiana University, vendors, and visitors who wish to participate in tailgate activities prior to an athletic event. Purpose of Policy Tailgating offers fans the chance to share in the collective excitement surrounding Southeastern athletic events. At Southeastern, we embrace all who wish to join us for athletic tailgating to show support for our student athletes while upholding safety and the preserving the beauty of our campus. The following document will outline the conditions and rules for tailgating at Southeastern Louisiana University. Applicability This policy will apply to student, staff, faculty, vendors, and visitors who are participating or attending the tailgate area. General • Although tailgating will be permitted on campus, certain areas may be designated to tailgate or designated for specific groups. • Tailgating will be limited to athletic events throughout campus. The University President reserves the right to designate areas of campus as non-tailgating locations. • Areas of campus may be reserved for specific university needs or events and will not be available for all game days. • Setting up, marking off, or holding a tailgate space in Friendship Circle without a tailgate prior approval from the Director for Athletics is prohibited. • Digging or any form of staking to secure tents is strictly prohibited without consent from the Director for the Physical Plant. • Amplified sound, including but not limited to radio station vans, live bands, DJs, televisions, sound systems, small battery-powered devices, is permitted with approval by the Director for Athletics. All sound must be terminated at the start of the game. Noise amplification and music must be kept to a reasonable level. Only Clean Music/Radio Version may be played at tailgate. • Soliciting or selling goods/services will not be permitted on stadium grounds without prior approval from the university. • Except as may be specifically authorized by University Police, the operation of drones/unmanned aircraft systems on University property is prohibited. • Smoking, vaping, or otherwise using marijuana, tetrahydrocannabinol (THC), its chemical derivatives, or other controlled dangerous substances is prohibited on University property. Louisiana R.S. 40:981.3 provides enhanced criminal penalties for certain controlled substance violations occurring on University property or within 2,000 feet of University property, including penalties of up to one year imprisonment and a fine of up to/n/n$1,000 for violations involving marijuana, THC, or its chemical derivatives. This prohibition does not apply to the lawful possession or use of medication in accordance with applicable state law. • Pets of any kind are prohibited. • Weapons of any kind are prohibited. • Tailgaters are responsible for the disposal of trash and debris in appropriate trash receptacles following the conclusion of each tailgate. Alcohol • Consumption of Alcoholic Beverages will be limited to persons of legal age or those exemptions under R.S. 14:93.10. • Drinking games, funneling, promoting binge drinking, or any other activity deemed by Southeastern Louisiana University as inappropriate is prohibited. • No oversized or common source containers of any type (including but not limited to kegs, punch bowls, beer balls, party balls) are permitted. • No individual glass containers are permitted. Cans and plastic bottles and cups are permitted. • Alcoholic beverages of any type cannot be brought into any Southeastern athletic facility from the outside. All persons entering any Southeastern Athletic facility are subject to search, and all items permitted must be in a clear bag. If alcohol is available for sale it can be purchased inside the facility. • No alcoholic beverage may leave the athletic facility and must be consumed or disposed of before leaving any Southeastern Athletic facility. • All alcoholic beverage possession, consumption, and distribution must comply with the Southeastern Louisiana University Alcohol Policy published by the Assistant Vice President for Student Affairs/Dean of Students. Open Fire Cooking, Grilling, Frying, etc.… • A fire extinguisher is recommended when grilling, boiling, frying, or any other type of cooking involving a flame. • Grills and other cooking devices must be at least 10 feet away from any structure. • Cooking under tents or in parking structures is prohibited. • Tabletop portable propane/butane cooking devices and fire pits are prohibited. • Coals must be fully extinguished and cold, then placed into the appropriate aluminum cans or a clean noncombustible container for disposal. Disposing of coals in university dumpsters is prohibited. • Food and beverages shall be for personal consumption only. Sales are prohibited. • Cooking oil must be disposed of properly and may not be poured down university drains. Generators • Electrical outlets are provided throughout the tailgating area. Personal generators are allowed but at no time should anyone run power cords to University buildings. • All electrical cords must be secured to prevent tripping hazards. • Generators must be at least 15 feet away from any structure. • Generators must be powered off at the start of the game. Tents • Tents may not obstruct sidewalks, walkways, or fire lanes. • Tents must be placed at least 10 feet away from buildings. • Tents must be secured with water ballasts or similar type anchor weights. Tents cannot be staked to the ground or anchored to buildings. Vehicles • Non-licensed vehicles are defined as a vehicle primarily used for the transportation of persons or cargo and generally recognizable as a passenger or utility type cart, vehicle, “off-road vehicle”, golf cart, club car, etc. Nonlicensed vehicles are permitted for tailgating but are not to be driven through pedestrian areas as otherwise identified in this policy. Such vehicles should be used as a mode of transportation to the tailgating location and parked for the duration of the tailgating activity except when moving to and from campus. Non-licensed vehicles are not to be parked on sidewalks, shall not be parked where it can cause damage to grass or landscape and must always yield to pedestrians. • Non-licensed vehicles considered “street legal” may be operated on campus streets and can be parked in an identified parking space. A non-licensed vehicle not parked in an identified parking space cannot be parked on sidewalks, shall not be parked where it can cause damage to grass or landscape and must always yield to pedestrians. • For purpose of this policy, Ned McGehee Drive (Friendship Circle) from McClimans Hall to Dyson Hall is considered pedestrian during tailgating activities. This area will be free from all moving vehicles including but not limited to cars, trucks, and non-licensed vehicles, with the exception of emergency and university vehicles. • Motor vehicles parked on Ned McGehee Drive after the closure will be subject to citation or towing at the owner’s expense. • All vehicles must yield to pedestrians at all times and adhere to all other street closures identified through signage, barricades, etc. • Ned McGehee will reopen to motor vehicles 30 minutes after kick off. • Parking is always prohibited in driveways, walkways, building exits, fire lanes or any place that would inhibit access of emergency vehicles. • Parking for RVs will be located on the west side of Dyson Hall and the overflow lot located on the North side of the practice field. Reservations • All reservations must be submitted through the Lion Spots website in order to request a spot. • Please note that signage will not be placed marking reservation spots. Fans are required to mark their own space for game day once approved. • Only the President of the student organization can request to reserve a green student organization tailgating spot. • Students/Fans who submit a request will receive a text message when their spot has been requested, approved or denied. • Students/Fans must request a spot for each individual game. • Deadlines to make reservations for each game is at least two days prior to game day. • Student Organizations may only request one spot for any one game at a time. All spots are on a first-come-firstserved basis. Conduct and Enforcement • All university policies are enforced throughout the campus, including the tailgating zones. University Police, designated security, or event management personnel has the authority to remove any individual or group who does not abide by campus policies. • Any individual acting in a disruptive, disrespectful, or disorderly manner may be asked to leave the premises or may be subject to removal or arrest by University Police. • Violation of tailgating conduct may result in immediate termination of tailgating privileges, and/or criminal prosecution. Students may also be charged with violation of the Student Code of Conduct. • Individuals who exhibit unruly or disruptive behavior, who are clearly intoxicated or who are determined to be under the age of 21 and in possession of alcohol, are subject to arrest, removal from the premises, and/or disciplinary action as defined by the Student Code of Conduct. • Motor vehicle rules will be enforced by the University Police under Title 32 of the Louisiana Revised Code. [End of Policy]
