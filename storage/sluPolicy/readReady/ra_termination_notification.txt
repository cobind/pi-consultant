Termination and Notification of Resident Assistants

All resident assistants hold their appointments at the pleasure or will of the University of Louisiana System. The annual approval of the budget and personnel documents designating the salary and other personnel benefits for administrative personnel shall not constitute an implied nor expressed agreement for continued employment throughout the fiscal year but are executed or approved solely for the purpose of budgeting and associated fiscal and administrative matters. Resident assistants who resign their positions or are terminated must reimburse the University the remaining pro rata portion of deferred payments. [End]
