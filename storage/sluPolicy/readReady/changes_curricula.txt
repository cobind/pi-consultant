Submission of Changes in Curricula

All curriculum changes, which include, but are not limited to, new degree programs, changes to degree programs, new courses, course changes, catalogue entry changes or course deletions, begin in the department requesting the change. The curriculum review process is managed in Curriculog. Faculty submitting curriculum changes within an academic department should discuss such changes with the department head and have those changes appropriately entered into Curriculog via the approved departmental curriculum chair, or appointee. It is incumbent on the departmental curriculum chair, department head and dean to ensure that appropriate Board of Regents, University of Louisiana and Southeastern policies are being followed. Proposals that do not follow these guidelines may be returned to the submitter or deleted from the system.

At the college level, the College Curriculum Committees are made up of the Departmental Curriculum Committee chairs within that college and the Dean of the college as an ex officio member. Approval by both the College Curriculum Committee and the Dean is required for the proposal to continue. If the curriculum change request affects general education, it is routed to the General Education and Innovation committee. If the curriculum change request affects a program in the College of Education, it is routed to the Council for Teacher Education. If the curriculum change affects a graduate program/course, it is routed to the Graduate Council. If the curriculum change affects a graduate program/course in the College of Education, it is routed through and approved by both committees. If the curriculum change request is for a new program or to change a program, it is routed to the SACSCOC liaison to determine whether or not the change is substantive. Proposals submitted to these committees are then submitted to the University Curriculum council chair.

As an advisory council to the Vice President for Academic Affairs, the Council reviews all proposals for undergraduate and graduate course and curriculum revisions and new academic programs. Council membership includes the faculty members chairing each of the college curriculum committees, the Chair of the Graduate Council, a Department Head representative and a student. Non-voting, ex officio members include the SACSCOC Liaison and representatives from Enrollment Services and the Library.

Approval by the University Curriculum Council forwards the proposal to Academic Affairs where final approval for all curriculum changes lies. After approval by Academic Affairs, proposals are archived and changes are entered into the general catalog by the Office of the Registrar. Proposals that are denied at any level are returned to the department submitting the request. The curriculum review cycle for the next academic year’s catalog begins on March 1 of each year and runs through the end of November. Proposals must be fully approved by the end of November to be added to the general catalog.
