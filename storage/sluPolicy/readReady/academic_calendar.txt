Academic Calendar

The Academic Calendar is recommended by the Calendar Committee to the Academic Affairs Council and approved by the President’s Staff. In addition to the faculty, staff, and student representation on the committee, input on the development of the Calendar is also sought from the faculty through the Faculty Senate and from students through the Student Government Association (SGA). The Calendar is available on Southeastern’s web site at: https://www.southeastern.edu/admin/registrar/dates/calendar/.
