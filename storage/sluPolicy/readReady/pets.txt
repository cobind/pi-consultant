Pets on Campus

Policy Statement

Southeastern Louisiana University is dedicated to fostering a welcoming and inclusive environment. In line with this commitment, the university ensures equal access to classrooms, facilities, public spaces, and housing, addressing the needs of community members requiring animal accommodations.

Purpose of Policy

This policy outlines guidelines for the admission and use of animals on campus.

Applicability

This policy applies to faculty, staff and students as well as visitors to the campus.

Policy Procedure

Pets are not allowed in University buildings, including classrooms, offices, laboratories, hallways, stairways, elevators, restrooms and common areas, except when accommodations are being made for students with disabilities (e.g. service animals). Faculty, staff and students should not bring pets to work, and at no time should a pet be left unattended on campus grounds.

[End of Policy]
