Termination of Employment of Student Workers

STUDENT WORKERS' RIGHTS & RESPONSIBILITIES

Students have the right to terminate employment at any time without negative impact on other financial aid. Students may request other employment if their current position is not satisfactory. Students have the responsibility to abide by the rules and regulations set forth by the employing department.

RELEASING A STUDENT EMPLOYEE

The decision to release any student is entirely up to the employing department. While the following Incident Reports may be helpful, they are not required; departments may terminate students at any time.
