Resident Assistantships

Purpose of Policy

The purpose of this policy is to clearly define the expectations and responsibilities associated with Resident Assistantships. This document aims to ensure that all Resident Assistants understand their roles and contribute effectively to the residential community and university.

Applicability

This policy applies to Resident Assistants and Resident Managers only.

Policy Procedure

Categories of Appointment Resident Assistant, Resident Assistant 1, and Resident Assistant 2: RAs must have a minimum 2.5 GPA and 30 credit hours. RAs must maintain a 2.5 or higher GPA and full-time status. RAs will have completed the required Leadership course with a “B” or better and received a favorable evaluation. A student on residence hall or disciplinary probation is not eligible for employment. RA1s must meet all of the RA requirements and also must have completed at least one semester as an RA. RA2s must meet all of the RA requirements and also must have completed a minimum of two semesters as an RA.

Resident Managers

Resident Manager: RMs must have completed a minimum of two semesters as a resident assistant and have experience working on a Housing Committee. RMs must have a minimum cumulative 2.5 GPA and must maintain full -time student status. RMs will have completed the required Leadership course with a “B” or better and received a favorable evaluation. A student on residence hall or disciplinary probation is not eligible for employment. Positions will be added as needed.

Terms of Appointment

All resident assistants and resident managers hold their appointments at the pleasure or will of the University of Louisiana System. 1. The employment term of resident assistants follows the University’s academic calendar from two weeks prior to the day classes start until one week following the last day of final exams. The employment term for resident managers will commence one week prior to resident assistants. 2. All resident assistants shall work an average of twenty (20) hours per week (Saturday through Friday). This may vary greatly from week to week. 3. All resident managers shall work up to twenty (20) hours per week in the Housing Office (Saturday through Friday) in addition to participating in an on-call rotation.

4. Resident assistants are employed on a semester -by-semester basis. The University do es not employ resident assistants for longer than one semester at a time. The department should make no commitments of employment lasting longer than one semester. Resident managers are employed for an academic year. 5. Federal regulations prohibit non -resident aliens from working more than twenty (20) hours per week during semesters or more than forty (40) hours per week during breaks. 6. Resident assistants and resident managers may not hold any other concurrent employment in the University. 7. Resident assistants and resident managers must attend the resident assistant orientation session held prior to the start of each semester. 8. Resident assistants and resident managers must attend regular pre-service and in-service training throughout the semester. 9. Resident assistants and resident managers are required to sign a job description stating the required duties. 10. A letter/email stating the dates of employment and remuneration will be sent to each resident assistant each semester. 11. Resident assistants and resident managers may not continue to be employed as resident assistants and resident managers after they graduate. 12. A resident assistant or resident manager who resigns or who will not be employed as a resident assistant or resident manager for the following semester must complete an Exit Check-out Form. Criminal background checks will be performed on all resident assistants and resident managers.

Academic Requirements

All resident assistants and resident managers must have been matriculated into the University prior to the beginning of their employment. 1. Resident assistants and resident managers must maintain “full -time” status. During the last semester prior to graduating, resident assistants may carry only the number of hours required to graduate with approval from t he director. 2. All resident assistants and resident managers must maintain a 2.5 GPA on a 4.0 scale. 3. Resident assistants and resident managers should carry no more than 18 hours per semester. 4. Resident assistants and resident managers seeking waivers of the course loads must secure the approval of the director prior to registering for classes. 5. Resident assistants and resident managers must complete the required Leadership course with a 3.0 or better on a 4.0 scale prior to being considered for a resident assistant or resident manager position. This requirement may be waived based on experience, but if hired, the employee must complete the class during the first semester of employment.

Appointment, Evaluation, and Continuation

1. University Housing must complete a Telephone Verification of Personal References for each new resident assistant and resident manager and submit the completed form, together with the other required employment materials, to Human Resources. 2. Resident assistant employment for new resident as sistants will not be processed until all employment materials (application for employment, pre -application, recommendation for employment, telephone verification of personal references, form W -4, form L -4, and copies of driver’s license and social security card) have been completed and received by the Human Resources Office. If the student does not have a driver’s license, contact Human Resources for acceptable substitutes. 3. All appointments and reappointments of resident assistants and resident managers are made by the director and/or associate director. These appointments are made with the understanding that the employment of the individual shall terminate unconditionally on the ending date of the semester for which they were appointed.

4. Supervisors may make written performance evaluations of resident assistants and resident managers and shall discuss these with the employee each semester. 5. Causes for discharge of resident assistants and resident managers include, but are not limited to, conduct seriously prejudicial to the employing department or to the University, such as infractions of the law or of commonly accepted standards of morality, failure to follow orders, violations of institutional or system rules and regulations, willful neglect of duty, ineffici ency, incompetence, or consistent or excessive tardiness or absenteeism. The foregoing enumeration of causes for discharge shall not be deemed exclusive. 6. Resident assistants or resident managers with grievances shall follow the University Grievance Procedure for Unclassified Staff.
