Key Policy

Policy Statement

We are committed to ensuring accessibility to our buildings while maintaining a safe and secure campus.

Purpose of Policy

The following policies are established to guide the request, issuance and use of keys for the university.

Applicability

This policy applies to all keys issued by the university.

Policy Procedure

• When requesting keys for students/faculty/staff members the requesting department must submit a completed service request form with the Department Head’s signature and the Building Coordinator’s signature to the Physical Plant Department. Departments may request room keys for students; however, building entrance keys will not be granted to students.

• Upon picking up the key(s), the students/faculty/staff member will sign the Key Issuance form for the key(s), thereby assuming the responsibility of the University Key Policy. He/she will be advised of his/her responsibility regarding keys at this time.

• Students/faculty/staff members will not be allowed to keep their keys when transferring or moving to a new office/building/department. Their current key(s) must be turned in before the new keys that are requested are issued to them.

• There will be an annual verification of keys that are still in the possession of assigned holders. This will assist the Physical Plant in being in compliance with the Office of Risk Management.

• Loaning of keys is prohibited.

• Unauthorized duplication of University keys could result in fines, disciplinary action, and/or prosecution.

• When a student/faculty/staff member leaves the employment of the University, he/she is to turn in his/her keys to the Physical Plant Services Department. Students who fail to return keys will be subject to the same restrictions as penalties as students who fail to honor their financial obligations to the University.

• In the event a key is lost, Physical Plant will charge a replacement fee per key according to the Key Level Chart. Additional fees may be charged for direct costs incurred by the University for items such as replacement of key cores ($50 per core), etc. Additional fees may also be applied for persons with multiple occurrences of lost keys. The student/faculty/staff’s Workday account will be charged for all expenses. In certain situations, the department may choose to cover the costs associated with the lost key(s). Such situations will require the approval of the budget unit head and funds must be available in the budget to cover the associated costs. The Physical Plant reserves the right to differ with the Department Head on whether the missing key poses a security threat.

Type of Key Replacement Cost

Level 1 High Risk Security $100.00 Level 2 Grandmaster $100.00 Level 3 General Access $75.00 Level 4 Building Master $75.00 Level 5 Department Master $50.00 Level 6 Room Key $25.00 Level 7 Building Entrance $25.00

Level One Key(s) – (Keys with the highest level of security)

When requesting level one key(s) for faculty/staff members, the requesting department must submit a completed service request for with the Department Head’s signature and the Building Coordinator’s signature to the Physical Plant Department along with a letter of justification. All level one key(s) will also require the approval of the Vice President for Administration and Finance and the Physical Plant Director. The Physical Plant will route the request for the approval of the Vice President. There will be a limit of three key(s) issued per core. A mandatory minimum fee of $100 per core replacement with newly issued keys will be charged to the department.
