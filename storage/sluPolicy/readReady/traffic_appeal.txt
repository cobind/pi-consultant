Parking and Traffic Appeals Policy

Policy Statement

Individuals wishing to appeal a parking or traffic citation must submit their appeal online through the Parking Portal within ten (10) calendar days of the citation’s issue date. The appeal must be completed in full for consideration. Appeals can be submitted 24/7 and will be reviewed by the Parking Appeal Review Committee, which consists of Faculty, Staff, and Student representatives. The committee meets once a month during the Fall and Spring semesters, and decisions can take up to 6 weeks.

Purpose of Policy

The purpose of this policy is to provide a clear and standardized process for individuals who wish to appeal parking or traffic citations. This ensures transparency, fairness, and consistency in handling appeals, while maintaining the integrity of parking regulations.

Applicability

This policy is applicable to: Faculty, staff, and students who hold a valid university parking permit and believe the citation was issued in error or under circumstances that warrant an appeal.

Policy Procedure

General Information

Any individual desiring to appeal a parking/traffic citation must submit his/her appeal online through the Parking Portal within ten (10) calendar days of the date of the citation(s). Online appeal forms are available and can be submitted 24hours a day, 7-days a week. The appeal will be reviewed by the Parking Appeal Review Committee.

• Before appealing a citation, please review the following instructions:

o To appeal a citation, first go to your parking Portal account, then to CITATIONS and CITATION APPEAL. You will need to put in your CITATION NUMBER and/ or LICENSE PLATE # to proceed. Please do not skip spaces between the letters and numbers of the plate. EX: P20003224, NDZ016 o If you receive a hand written parking citation, you will need to put three zeros in front of the citation number and then license plate number. EX: 000765226. If it is a Campus Traffic Citation, you will need to put in two zeros in front of the number and then your license plate number. EX: 001205552. o Once a citation is issued, you have 10 calendar days to appeal it. If you receive a citation it can be appealed on the following business day.

• The citation being appealed will remain on the individual’s account until after a decision has been rendered at which time, if the appeal is approved by the Parking Appeal Review Committee, the citation fee will be removed from the individual’s account. You may check the status of your appeal in your Parking Portal at any time by going online the same as appealing a citation.

• An appeal will not be considered if one or both of the following administrative requirements are not met:

o The appeal must be submitted within the ten (10) calendar day period mentioned above. o The appeal form must be properly completed.

• Only the individual who purchased the permit can appeal a citation issued against it. In the event the vehicle does not display a parking permit, only the individual (student and/or employee) who operates the vehicle on campus may appeal a citation(s) issued to the vehicle.

• Anyone receiving a citation has the right to appeal the citation under the following conditions:

o The appeal must be made within 10 calendar days from the citation issue date. o All appeals must be filed online through the Parking Portal o One “courtesy” void of a citation for Unregistered Vehicle or Backwards in space will be allowed for persons who purchase a parking permit. Presentation of the citation must be made at Transportation Services. (Limit one per academic year, some restrictions apply). o All outstanding fines must be paid before a citation can be appealed. o Only the individual who purchases the permit can appeal a citation issued against it. o In the event the vehicle does not display a parking permit, only the individual (student and/or employee) who operates the vehicle on campus may appeal a citation(s) issued to the vehicle.

Appeals Process

• Appellant submits appeal.

• The citation in question is marked on the appellant’s account to show it is under appeal.

• The Parking Appeal Review Committee will review the appeal and will render a decision.

• The Parking Appeal Review Committee will consist of representatives of Faculty, Staff, and Students. This committee will meet once a month during the Fall and Spring Semesters.

• Once a decision is rendered the appellant will receive an email notifying them of the results of the appeal.

• If the appeal is found valid the ticket will be voided. If the appeal is mitigated or denied, the appellant is billed.

• All decisions made by the Parking Appeal Review Committee are final; this process may take up to 6 weeks to complete.

Grounds for Appeal

• Appeal only when you have valid grounds. Limit your appeal to the circumstances in which the parking ticket was issued in error. Although all parking and traffic citation appeals will be considered, if the facts show that you committed a violation of the parking rules, your appeal will most likely be denied regardless of the reason or excuse. The issue of your appeal is not whether the parking regulations are fair, but whether you violated those regulations. Below are examples of seldom granted appeals:

o Lack of knowledge/understanding of parking regulations o Legal parking space was unavailable o Improper display or failure to display the current parking permit o Inclement weather

o Late arrival for class, meeting, appointment o Parking in a handicap space without the proper State and University permits o Parked only for a short time o Disagreement with or inability to pay the amount of the fine o Did not see a parking sign o Left my permit in another vehicle.
