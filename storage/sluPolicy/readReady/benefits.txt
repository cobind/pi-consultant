Benefits Programs

Southeastern offers a wide variety of benefit programs to include retirement, insurance, retirement savings plans annuities, flexible spending accounts, premium Conversion, and other miscellaneous benefits. Employee participation is voluntary for all benefit programs other than retirement. The University pays a portion of both retirement and State Group Benefits Health Insurance and Life Insurance premiums. Newly hired employees should be aware that there is a waiting period for most coverage.

Below is a list of benefits currently offered through Human Resources. Miscellaneous benefit programs are added and removed by recommendations of the Employee Advisory Committee. The list is subject to change. Contact the Benefits Office at benefits@southeastern.edu for a current list of offerings and vendors.
