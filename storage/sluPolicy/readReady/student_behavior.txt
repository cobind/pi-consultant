Student Behavior in the Classroom

Policy Statement

Southeastern Louisiana University intends to maintain a respectful classroom environment conducive to learning. This policy is designed to establish clear expectations for student behavior, promote positive interactions, and ensure the safety and well-being of all students and educators.

Purpose of Policy

This policy aims to foster an atmosphere of respect, inclusivity, and mutual consideration among students, faculty, and staff. By defining clear expectations for appropriate behavior, this policy aims to facilitate an effective teaching and learning environment that ensures all individuals' physical and emotional safety in the classroom. Additionally, this policy provides a structured framework for addressing and managing behavioral issues fairly and consistently . Through open communication and collaboratio n between students and educators, this policy addresses behavior -related concerns effectively, promoting a harmonious and productive educational experience at Southeastern Louisiana University.

Applicability

This policy applies to all students and covers behavior within the educational setting whether in-person and/or virtual.

Policy Procedure

Whenever a student’s behavior in class is deemed by the instructor to be disruptive, the student should be promptly informed of such and requested to cease the behavior. Notification and request may occur in class or another time and place deemed appropriate by the faculty member (e.g., the faculty member’s office). At this time, the student should also be notified that failure to comply with the faculty member’s request may result in the student being subject to disciplinary action by the Office of Student Advocacy and Accountability under the Student Code of Conduct.

If the student does not cease the disruptive behavior, the student may be dismissed from class. The student may attend subsequent classes unless the disruptive behavior persists. If the student refuses the faculty member’s request to cease the disruptive behavior, the University Police may be requested to remove the student. Faculty also should report incidents of disruptive behavior to the Office of Student Advocacy and Accountability, the department head, and the Dean.

The University Police should be involved only after all reasonable classroom management efforts have failed. However, if at any time a faculty member believes that a student poses a physical threat, the University Police should be contacted immediately
