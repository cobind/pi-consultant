Responsible Office: Human Resources Date Reviewed (no substantive changes): 01/29/2024 Union Organization All classified employees are eligible, but not required, to join Local No. 489 of the American Federation of State, County, and Municipal Employees, AFL-CIO. Employees supervising union members are to review the union agreement so as not to violate any of its provisions. Union members are also expected to act in good faith in abiding by the provisions of the agreement. For more information on the agreement between Southeastern Louisiana University and Local 489 of the American Federation of State, Country, and Municipal Employees, AFL-CIO, please contact the Human Resources Office or any union officer.
