Rewards and Recognition Program – Unclassified Employees Handbook

Southeastern Louisiana University management fully supports and recognizes outstanding performance and service to the University. Southeastern is committed, insofar as budgetary constraints allow, to improving our recognition and rewards program.

No single award, nor the sum of multiple awards, shall total more than 10% of any employee’s base salary in a fiscal year. Amounts listed are subject to periodic review and may change in the future depending upon available funding. If monetary, such rewards must be lump sum and shall not be a part of the employees’ base pay.

Applicability

This applies to unclassified employees.

Procedure

20 Years of Service –lapel pin (est. value $2.14) 25 Years of Service – shadow box frame & medallion (est. value $58.70) 30 Years of Service – Southeastern logo watch (est. value $102.45) 35 Years of Service – pendulum wall clock (est. value $265.00) 40 Years of Service – Southeastern blazer (est. value $250.00) or crystal Plaque (est. value $115.00) 45 Years of Service – Southeastern logo ring (est. value $150) 50 Years of Service – Bronze Plaque w/President’s Seal (est. value $209. 00)

Procedure for Service Awards Program

• Service Awards recipient list will be compiled by Human Resources; no application is required.

• Service award recipients will be selected by the Human Resources Office Staff based on years of Southeastern service. The Vice President for Administration and Finance is responsible for certifying if funds are available to pay the recommended awards.
