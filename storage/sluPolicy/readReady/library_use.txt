Use of Linus A. Sims Memorial Library by Faculty and Staff

The Linus A. Sims Memorial Library provides a full range of resources and services to support teaching and scholarship. In addition to books, periodicals, media, and government documents, the Library supports a wide array of other services including interlibrary loan, electronic reserves, electronic database searching, and document retrieval. Library faculty collaborate with classroom instructors in an extensive program of information literacy instruction, tailored to the needs of individual courses. Librarians are also available to introduce new resources in the disciplines and help develop search strategies for complex topics in individual research consultations. Reference questions are answered in person, by phone, by email, by text message or live chat. Detailed information about Library resources and services can be found on the Southeastern web site at http://www.southeastern.edu/library/.
