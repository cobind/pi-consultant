Employee Safety Meetings

Policy Statement

Southeastern Louisiana University adheres to all requirements of the Louisiana Office of Risk Management, including quarterly safety meetings.

Purpose of Policy

This is a requirement of the Louisiana Office of Risk Management.

Applicability

All state employees are required to participate in safety meetings.

Policy Procedure

On a quarterly basis, Southeastern’s Environmental Health & Safety Office notifies all employees that the quarterly topic is available through Canvas. The ultimate responsibility lies with the Budget Unit Head to make sure all employees complete the training.

[End of Policy]
