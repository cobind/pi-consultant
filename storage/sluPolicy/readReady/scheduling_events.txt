Scheduling Evens on Campus

Policy Statement

The University has procedures in place to assist with scheduling and hosting events on campus. The Registration of Activities process is the central guide for scheduling events on campus.

Purpose of Policy

The purpose of this policy is to establish clear, consistent, and equitable expectations for reserving University-owned spaces. This policy offers a streamline process for the use of University shared spaces to ensure consistency, maintain safety, and provide accessibility for events that align with the University’s mission.

Applicability

This policy applies to events sponsored by all departments, student organizations, faculty, staff, and students.

Policy Procedure

The Dean of Students Office manages the Registration of Activities process for university departments and registered student organizations to facilitate event planning; university spaces cannot be reserved for individual use. All campus groups must review the guidelines and complete the Registration of Activities form available at www.southeastern.edu/roa to secure campus facilities. This form must be submitted to the Dean of Students Office 14 business days prior to an event. Use of this link in no way guarantees the requester that the facility and/or services will be available or that the event is approved. Other relevant policies and procedures are as follows:

1. Off Campus Events Student events that take place Off-Campus are not required to be registered through the Registration of Activities platform unless one of the following applies:

• The organization is utilizing University funds such as departmental funds, SGA grants, Aramark grant, etc.

• The event is a part of Fraternity and Sorority new member intake processes. If these examples apply, the organization must complete the Registration of Activities Form and receive the proper approvals before hosting the off-campus event. The Dean of Students Office provides General Guidelines for student sponsored events occurring off campus available at https://www.southeastern.edu/admin/stu_dev/offcampusevents/

2. Events Involving Alcohol The possession, use, and sale of alcohol on campus is permissible in accordance with the provisions of federal and state laws, local laws and ordinances, and University regulations outlined in the Alcohol Policy.

3. External Events All external event requests and space bookings must be submitted through Event and Conference Services (Phone: (985) 549-2041; Email: conf_services@southeastern.edu).

4. Demonstration and Assembly The University’s Policy on Public Speech, Assembly, and Demonstrations Policy ensures that Southeastern faculty, staff, students, and outside groups have the freedom to assemble and engage in spontaneous expressive activity, provided it is lawful, does not disrupt the functioning of the institution, and participants do not violate other university policies.

5. Event Updates & Cancellations Update or cancellation requests from event organizers will be considered and managed in a timely manner. Departments and Student Organizations should contact the Dean of Students Office to request event changes or cancellations (Phone: (985) 549-3792; Email: deanofstudents@southeastern.edu). The Dean of Students Office is not responsible for alerting possible event attendees of event cancellations. Event organizers/sponsors should take proactive steps to alert possible event attendees through available channels.

Facilities are available on a space available basis to host events, and the University reserves the right to charge user fees for certain facilities used. All usage must comply with this policy, the Student Code of Conduct, and relevant laws and regulations. Additional details about the Registration of Activities process, including related information and forms, can be found at www.southeastern.edu/roa.
